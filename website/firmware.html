FIRMWARE INSTALLER

LATEST: v1.3.0

Connect your Animatrix ESP32-S3 via USB to install or recover the firmware directly from your browser.

⚠️ DATA LOSS WARNING:

Flashing via USB performs a complete factory reset. All custom animations and settings saved on your helmet will be permanently erased. If you need to keep your files, please use the Local Wi-Fi Update method in the embedded dashboard instead.

Installation Requirements:
  • Must use a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera, or Brave).
  • Ensure your USB cable supports data transfer (some are power-only).
  • Close any serial monitors or other programs using the COM port.
